## Full description (≤4000 chars)

```
Amanah is a personal finance app built for Muslims. Track expenses, plan
budgets, and calculate Zakat — entirely on-device, without ads or
account sign-ups.

WHY AMANAH

Most finance apps are built around interest-bearing accounts and ad-
funded data harvesting. Amanah respects Islamic finance principles
and your privacy: no riba framing, no data shared with third parties,
no subscriptions.

ISLAMIC FEATURES

- Zakat calculator: input your zakatable assets and Amanah computes
  the due amount against the current nisab (gold + silver spot
  prices auto-refresh daily, opt-in). Hawl tracking.

- Asnaf tracker: log Sadaqa and Zakat distribution across the eight
  asnaf categories (faqir, miskin, amilin, mu'allaf, riqab,
  gharimin, fi sabilillah, ibn al-sabil).

- Halal status on transactions: tag merchants halal / haram /
  mushbooh. Quick filter on the Activity tab.

- Riba flag per account: mark interest-bearing accounts. Amanah
  surfaces a footer reminder to consult a scholar for the right
  zakat treatment.

- Hijri-first hero: Hijri date in large Arabic numerals, Gregorian
  underneath. Islamic months named in Arabic for the locale.

- Prayer-time aware Ramadan mode: iftar reminders, sunset-aware
  hour bands.

- Mazhab + prayer-time method picker for accurate Adhan timings via
  the open-source Adhan library.

CORE FINANCE

- Smart Add: type "12 coffee" and Amanah predicts the category —
  4-layer model (note, your merchant memory, time of day, fallback).

- Recurring bills, budgets, goals, debt tracker.

- Net worth chart: 24 months of history.

- Income vs spending insights, monthly recap, PDF reports.

- 50 locales. Right-to-left layouts for Arabic, Urdu, Persian.

- Demo profile bundled — explore the full app with two years of
  realistic sample data before adding a single real transaction.

PRIVACY

All financial data stays on your device. No backend. No login. No
ads. Opt-in only: daily FX rates from a public exchange-rate
provider, daily gold/silver spot from goldapi.io.

EXPORT

Full JSON / CSV export and restore. PDF monthly and yearly reports.

OPEN ROADMAP

Cloud sync, OCR receipt scanning, home-screen widgets are planned
for v1.1+.

—

Privacy: https://aqsaqal.github.io/moneyapp/amanah/privacy/
Terms:   https://aqsaqal.github.io/moneyapp/amanah/terms/
Support: ak.saken@gmail.com
```
