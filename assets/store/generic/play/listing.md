## Full description (≤4000 chars)

```
Flow is a personal finance app that respects your privacy. Your money data
stays on your device. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking SDKs.

WHY FLOW

Most finance apps are a privacy black box — they read your transactions,
sell your spending patterns, and lock features behind subscriptions. Flow
doesn't. Everything works offline. The app costs nothing.

WHAT'S INSIDE

- Smart Add: type "12 coffee" and Flow predicts the category from a
  4-layer model — note keyword, your merchant memory, the time of day,
  and a fallback. Median add is 3 taps.

- Recurring bills: track rent, subscriptions, salary. The Plan tab
  shows what's coming, what's paid, and what's left in your monthly
  Disposable.

- Budgets per category: a soft warning at 80%, a stop sign at 100%.
  Rollover supported.

- Net worth chart: 24 months of history, four time windows, drill
  into any account.

- Insights: month-over-month deltas per category, income vs. spending
  by month, spike alerts when a category is 2σ above your rolling mean.

- Multi-currency: hold accounts in different currencies, see totals
  converted to your display currency. FX rates refresh daily from a
  public provider — opt-in.

- 50 locales. Right-to-left layouts for Arabic, Hebrew, Persian, Urdu.

- Demo profile bundled — explore the full app with two years of
  realistic sample data before adding a single real transaction.

PRIVACY

Your data is stored only on your device (SQLite + MMKV). No backend.
No login. No advertising network. No telemetry by default. Optional:
FX rates fetched once per day from a public exchange-rate service; you
can switch this off.

EXPORT

Export your full history as JSON or CSV at any time. Restore from a
prior JSON export to a fresh install. Monthly and yearly PDF reports.

OPEN ROADMAP

Cloud sync, OCR receipt scanning, home-screen widgets, and Apple Watch
/ Wear OS companions are tracked for v1.1+.
```
